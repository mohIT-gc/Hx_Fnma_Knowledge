# MyServices Organizational Contacts

Use this document to answer ownership and organizational-routing questions for the MyServices product area.

## Core contacts

- Director: Director of Servicing Platforms
- Product manager: Senior Product Manager, MyServices
- Delivery lead: Delivery Lead, MyServices Platform
- Engineering manager: Engineering Manager, MyServices Platform

## Team structure

- MyServices Platform owns product delivery and service reliability.
- Partner integrations work with MyServices Platform on upstream and downstream contracts.
- Operations support teams consume MyServices APIs and dashboard outputs.

## Escalation path

1. Ask in #myservices-eng for development and onboarding questions.
2. Contact the Engineering Manager for staffing, priorities, or delivery ownership.
3. Route production incidents through the on-call process and incident commander.
