# MyServices Product Overview

MyServices is the servicing workflow platform for internal operators and partner-facing service teams.

## What the product does

- Manages customer servicing cases and work queues.
- Coordinates status updates across downstream servicing systems.
- Exposes internal APIs used by support tooling and operational dashboards.
